Jacket 1.0, release candidate 1. New in this version:
• Read-aloud keeps playing with the screen off, with lock-screen and headphone controls and a sleep timer
• Bookmarks, highlights with notes, and search inside EPUBs
• Line spacing, margins, justification and volume-key page turns
• Finished shelf, end-of-book screen, library search and sorting
• Backup export and import

Known issue: read-aloud for plain-text books stops when you leave the app.
