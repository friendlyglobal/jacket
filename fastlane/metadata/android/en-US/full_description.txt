Jacket is a free, open-source e-reader that lets your foldable wear the book you're reading. While you read inside, the outer screen shows the book's cover edge to edge, like album art. When you'd rather listen, natural voices read any book aloud, right on your phone. It works on any Android phone, and the cover features come into their own on foldables.

THE COVER, ON THE OUTSIDE
• Book-style foldables: the cover fills the outer screen while you read inside, following your reading live: New, Reading, Almost there, Listening, Finished, and a low-power ambient state.
• If you like, the dimmed cover shows one of your highlights.
• Cover when closed: fold the phone shut on an open book and the cover stays on, with a player along the bottom so you can pause or skip without unfolding.
• Galaxy Z Flip: a cover-screen widget. Motorola Razr: open Jacket on the external display.

LISTEN
• Natural voices speak 31 languages, right on your phone. The voice your book needs downloads once, the first time you listen, and then works without the internet. Your phone's own voice reads the rest.
• Each word lights up on the page as it's spoken, and the page turns with the voice.
• Tap any sentence to hear it from there. Stop, and next time it carries on from the sentence you left.
• Keeps going with the screen off, with lock-screen and headphone controls.
• Speed from 0.8× to 2×, and a sleep timer for 15, 30 or 60 minutes or the end of the chapter.

READ
• EPUB, PDF, CBZ comics and plain text from your own files, through Add books, Open with or Share.
• Real EPUB pages, two side by side when unfolded, or scrolling.
• Page turns that follow your finger: Paper, Curl, Cards or Slide.
• Contents, bookmarks, highlights in five colors, notes, and search inside EPUBs.
• Literata, Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic fonts; text size, line spacing, margins and justification; four page colors.
• Pinch or double-tap to zoom PDFs and comics.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Free books: 114 classics in 16 languages whose copyright has run out, ready to download through your browser.
• Collections for a book club, a trip or a genre.
• Give any book a new cover: a photo, or a design in your colors.
• Widgets: your book's cover, a player, your shelf, and your reading streak.
• Today's reading time and your streak, time left in the chapter, and a watched folder that adds new books.

DAILY COMFORTS
• Tabletop mode: half-folded, the page sits above the fold and the controls below.
• Edge swipes for brightness and warmth, and dark pages at night.
• Volume keys turn pages.
• Share a highlight as a quote, or all of a book's notes at once.
• A "Back" pill returns you to your page after a jump.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Italian, German, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

PRIVACY
• Online for one thing only: Jacket downloads a voice from GitHub, once, when you say yes. Nothing is uploaded, and the books you open in it can't reach the network. The voices speak on your phone itself.
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Backup to a file you choose: your books list, places, bookmarks, highlights and settings (not the book files themselves).
• Free and open source under the Apache License 2.0: https://github.com/friendlyglobal/jacket

GOOD TO KNOW
• Outer-screen support depends on the phone; without one, those options are hidden.
• DRM-protected books can't be opened.
• PDFs and comics aren't read aloud, and read-aloud for plain-text books stops when you leave the app.
